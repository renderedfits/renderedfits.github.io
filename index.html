Rendered Fits open data and tools

Rendered Fits makes a sizing-led virtual try-on app for Shopify fashion brands. We publish our research data under CC BY 4.0.

Data

Each dataset has a DOI on Zenodo, so you can cite it directly. Files and citation details: github.com/renderedfits/fashion-sizing-open-data. Summary of all the figures: Clothing Size and Fit Statistics 2026.

  • Shopify Size Chart Report 2026: 829 fashion stores, 107,138 sized products. Only 31.6% of stores have a size chart shoppers (or AI) can read as text. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24165866
  • Sizing information by garment category: 459 stores, 74,111 sized products. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24165871
  • AI Sizing Study 2026: what AI shopping assistants receive about size from 1,383 UK and US Shopify clothing stores (anonymised). DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24165873
  • UK size 12 measurements: bust, waist and hip ranges for UK women's sizes 8 to 18 across 26 retailer size charts. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24165875
  • Fashion Returns Index 2026

Also on Hugging Face and Kaggle. Machine-readable catalogue: catalog.json.
